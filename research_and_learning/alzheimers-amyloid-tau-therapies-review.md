## When to use

Use for a literature-based comparison of disease mechanisms, protein-targeted antibody therapies, and clinical and imaging evidence in Alzheimer's disease.

## Inputs

This saved version has no placeholders. Optionally specify a publication cutoff date, the time window meant by "recent," the intended audience, and citation style.

## Prompt

```text
Analyze the mechanisms of amyloid ẞ and tau propagation in Alzheimer's disease. Contrast monoclonal antibody therapies targeting each protein, and critically appraise cognitive and imaging outcomes from recent phase 3 trials. Include relevent tables and visualizations
```

## Usage notes

- Define "recent" with a date range when running the prompt and cite the trial publications or registry records supporting the review.
- Check which therapies have phase 3 evidence available at the review date. Distinguish earlier-phase findings and ongoing trials from completed phase 3 results, and identify evidence gaps explicitly.
- For comparison tables, consider trial population, intervention, comparator, endpoints, follow-up, effect sizes, uncertainty, and adverse events. Label the sources and units in visualizations.
- The supplied character "ẞ" and spelling "relevent" are preserved. For reuse, amyloid beta is conventionally written "amyloid β" and the spelling is "relevant."
